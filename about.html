ABOUT CHRIS & ZTIME

MORE THAN A NICKNAME.

My name is Chris Ziegler, and ZTime started as my nickname in high school. What began as something my friends called me is now something I want to turn into much more.

WHERE IT STARTED

What is ZTime?

ZTime was my nickname in high school. On the football field, my friends would yell, “WHAT TIME IS IT?!” and the answer was always “ZTIME — LET'S GET IT!”

Now I am taking that nickname and building it into something bigger. I want ZTime to become a household name that brings music, fitness, motivation, and community together in one place.

THE VISION

Music and fitness together

My goal is for ZTime to give people a place where they can listen to music, create their own playlists, listen to the radio, and use music as part of their fitness journey.

I also want people to be able to watch fitness videos, track their workouts and progress, set fitness goals, and challenge themselves along the way.

THE COMMUNITY

Challenge yourself and each other

Fitness does not always have to be something you do by yourself. ZTime is meant to support individual goals while also giving people a way to work toward challenges with friends or with a larger community.

Whether someone is trying to stay consistent, hit a new goal, or just find motivation to keep moving, I want ZTime to be a place that helps them push themselves.
