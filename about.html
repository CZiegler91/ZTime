ABOUT ZTIME

MORE THAN MUSIC.

ZTime is a fitness and music website concept designed to bring workouts, playlists, radio, progress tracking, and community features into one place.

THE IDEA

Your time. Your zone.

The goal of ZTime is to create one platform where users can listen to music, organize playlists, track workouts, monitor progress, and connect with a community.

This version was created as a web development course project and will continue to grow as new features are added.
