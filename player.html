ABOUT THE PLAYER

Music That Moves With You

The ZTime Player demonstrates track selection, play and pause controls, and a queue alongside your remembered local radio selection. The training mix controls are a visual demo and do not control the official embedded radio player.

PLAYER GUIDE

Explore the listening experience

01

Choose a track

Select a title in Up Next to update the featured track, or use the previous and next buttons to move through the three sample selections. The highlighted queue item shows which track is selected.

02

Try the controls

Play and pause start or stop the visual progress demonstration. These are sample titles and a simulated timeline; no audio files or streaming service are connected. The player remains a preview of the ZTime idea.

03

Plan your real soundtrack

Use the playlist page to think through the mood and order of your session. Bring those ideas to your preferred music app today, then return to ZTime to record the work you completed.
