PLAN THE SOUNDTRACK

Build a mix with a beginning, middle, and finish

01

Ease into the session

Start your playlist with familiar tracks that help you settle in. Leave room for preparation before the songs you associate with your hardest work. The opening should make it easy to get started.

02

Find your working rhythm

Group songs with a similar feel for the main part of your session. A steady mix can suit repetitive activity, while a few high-energy favorites can mark a change in your routine. Choose what fits your taste.

03

Leave room to wind down

Finish with tracks that feel less demanding. A deliberate ending gives your playlist a clear shape and makes it easier to reuse. After listening, move or remove any song that interrupted the flow.

A playlist worth repeating

Give your mix a useful name such as “Evening Walk” or “Strength Favorites.” Keep a short core of songs you enjoy, then swap a few when the mix feels stale. Playlist creation is a future feature; these ideas can guide a mix in the music service you already use.
