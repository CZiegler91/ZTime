DISCOVER YOUR SOUND

Choose a station for the moment

01

Energy for training

Workout Radio is the all-around concept for a session with variety. Rock puts guitars and drums at the center; Hip-Hop emphasizes rhythm and flow. Try a different direction when your usual soundtrack feels repetitive.

02

Space for focus

The Focus concept is intended for moments when you want music in the background. Think about whether vocals pull your attention away from the task, and choose the sound that lets you concentrate.

03

Turn discovery into a favorite

When a new song catches your attention, note its title and artist in your own music app. Build a small collection of discoveries and decide later which ones belong in your regular workout mix.

Radio or playlist?

A playlist gives you a familiar order and specific song choices. A station adds discovery and less predictable variety. ZTime’s station cards describe the planned experience; they do not currently stream audio. Visit the Player demo to explore the control layout.
