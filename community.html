SHOW UP FOR EACH OTHER

Bring something useful to the conversation

01

Share one practical detail

Describe what you tried and why it worked for you. A specific playlist idea, a way to organize your log, or a simple preparation habit gives someone else a clear starting point. Personal experiences can differ.

02

Try a consistency challenge

Choose a small activity to repeat during the week and invite a friend to check in with you. Share what you completed and what you learned. The purpose is encouragement, with room for different schedules and abilities.

03

Keep the space welcoming

Celebrate effort, ask respectful questions, and avoid comparing someone else’s starting point with your own. Leave private information out of posts and credit the creator when discussing someone else’s music or ideas.
